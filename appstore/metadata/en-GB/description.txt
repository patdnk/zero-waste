Zero Waste helps you use up the food you already have, before it goes in the bin.

SCAN THE LABEL, SKIP THE TYPING
Point your camera at a best-before or use-by date and Zero Waste reads it for you. Scan the barcode too and the product name fills itself in. Labels are read on your phone.

KNOW WHAT TO USE FIRST
Your fridge, freezer and cupboard are sorted by urgency. The item that needs eating first sits at the top with a big countdown and one-tap Used it, Freeze or Recipes.

A NUDGE BEFORE IT GOES OFF
Choose how many days' notice you want and what time of day. You'll get a reminder before the date, one on the day, and one if something slips past.

COOK WHAT'S ABOUT TO EXPIRE
Recipes are ranked by what's already in your fridge, with food that's about to go off counted first. Each recipe shows what you have, what's going off and what's missing.

MADE FOR REAL KITCHENS
• Fridge, freezer and cupboard in one place
• Move food to the freezer and add three months in one tap
• Mark things as used, or put them back
• Search everything you've stored
• No account needed: your food list stays on your device

Waste less. Cook more.
